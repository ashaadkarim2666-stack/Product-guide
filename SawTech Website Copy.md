 Core Features:

*  Inbound Calls

For inbound calls, the voice agent handles incoming calls on behalf of the support team and intelligently responds to the given queries based on the pre-defined workflows. It also utilizes custom logic to answer the call and provide a response based on that logic. The AI voice agent can capture caller input, route calls, or even provide answers using connected tools.

* Outbound Calls

The AI voice agent is capable of initiating calls based on several triggers. For instance, if there is an appointment reminder or a follow-up, the agent will dial calls, helping businesses automate their workflow and avoid delays in operations. It utilizes voice synthesis to interact naturally with callers. One of the key features of our AI voice agent is that it can be integrated with CRMs or business systems for personalized interactions.
