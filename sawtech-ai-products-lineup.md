# AI Voice Agents \- SawTech AI Product Lineup

# **1\. OUTBOUND AI VOICE AGENTS**

SawTech enables businesses to streamline their operations with incredible automation agents that resolve queries and handle various tasks. Modern breakthroughs in technology have transformed the landscape of our digital world, and with such advancements, businesses resort to modern means of communication and management. After taking the nature of such demands into consideration, we have developed AI agents, designed specifically to initiate calls and take proactive action.

## **a. Cold Call Sales Agent / Lead Qualification Agent**

Designed to call prospects from a given lead list and intelligently pitch your product or services, and qualify the interest. It can automatically book meetings or transfer hot leads to humans, as well as reach out to the incoming leads from a form or an ad campaign. The agent assists businesses by asking predefined questions to help you avoid the hassle. And on top of that, it updates the CRM by forwarding all the qualified leads to the sales department.

## **c. Appointment Reminder Agent**

The agent takes charge of calling customers before their scheduled appointments to confirm or reschedule their appointments. It has been synced with various calendar tools such as Google Calendar and Calendly. 

## **d. Payment Reminder Agent**

Featured to provide follow-ups on unpaid invoices and send follow-up links by SMS or email. It can further escalate to a human if need be.

## **e. Customer Survey Agent**

It is just the perfect AI assistant to call customers after purchase/service and ask to offer customers’ feedback on your behalf, and give a satisfaction score. The survey agent is capable enough to record responses in CRM as well as the analytics dashboard.

## **f. Event Invitation/Confirmation Agent**

The event invitation and confirmation agent is an automated AI assistant that invites leads or customers to webinars or events and verifies their attendance. After the verification, it sends calendar invites to the respective customers.

# **2\. INBOUND AI VOICE AGENTS**

SawTech provides an efficient AI voice agent for inbound calls, developed to answer incoming calls and handle them professionally.

##  **a. Customer Support Agent**

Our customer support agent deals with customer calls and handles queries related to questions about hours, refunds, and their status. If the queries are complex, it automatically routes such cases to a human responder. One of the key benefits of this agent is that it works 24/7 and responds immediately.

##  **b. Order Tracking Agent**

It takes a customer’s order number and assists them regarding the status of their order and shipping. The agent is integrated with an impactful e-commerce backend like Shopify and WooCommerce.

##  **c. Service Booking Agent**

It can accept bookings over the phone and suggest the available timeslots. It can also confirm the bookings and send confirmation via SMS and email.

##  **d. Information Hotline Agent**

Designed to answer calls and brief customers regarding the company or product information. The agent fetches data from the knowledge base of the FAQ database to give information to the caller. It can also send follow-ups through SMS using a link or the necessary details.

##  **f. After-Hours Assistant**

Our newly introduced after-hours assistant receives customer calls after business hours. It forwards a query or handles basic issues to resolve them. After handling the matter, it sends a summary to the relevant team
